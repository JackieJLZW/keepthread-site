Why does ChatGPT & DeepSeek LaTeX break in Obsidian?

1. Obsidian expects $ and $$, not \( \) / \[ \]

ChatGPT and DeepSeek frequently output standard LaTeX delimiters \(x^2\) and \[ E = mc^2 \]. Obsidian's MathJax renderer only parses $x^2$ and $$...$$, leaving raw backslashes visible in your notes.

2. Spaces inside inline $ x + y $ break MathJax

Even when dollar signs are present, a space right after the opening $ or before the closing $ causes Obsidian to treat it as plain currency text instead of math. Our converter trims those automatically.

3. Reasoning blocks & UI chrome clutter your Vault

DeepSeek-R1 and local reasoning models emit <think>...</think> blocks, and copying from web chat UIs drags in stray Copy code lines. Turning reasoning into > [!note]- Thinking Process keeps notes clean and foldable.
