KeepThread Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection for Your Conversations

KeepThread is engineered as a 100% local-first browser extension. We do not operate a backend server that receives, stores, logs, or analyzes your AI prompts, responses, browsing history, or exported Markdown files.

2. Where Your Data Lives

  • Browser Local Storage & IndexedDB: Archived conversations, search indexes, user preferences, and your selected local folder handle are stored exclusively on your own device inside Chrome/Edge local storage and IndexedDB.
  • Local Obsidian Vault Folder: When you explicitly link a local folder via the browser File System Access API, KeepThread writes Markdown files directly to your machine. You can revoke access or unlink the folder at any time.

3. Minimal Host Permissions

KeepThread never requests <all_urls> permissions. Content scripts are strictly scoped to the 5 supported AI chat domains (chatgpt.com / chat.openai.com, gemini.google.com, perplexity.ai, chat.deepseek.com, and grok.com).

4. Optional Pro License Verification

If you choose to purchase and activate a Lifetime Pro license key, KeepThread communicates directly with Dodo Payments (live.dodopayments.com) solely to activate, validate, or deactivate your license key and device label. No conversation text or browsing URLs are ever included in license requests. Payment details are handled directly by Dodo Payments as our Merchant of Record.

5. Contact

Questions or privacy inquiries? Contact us at support@jianlanzhiwei.com.
