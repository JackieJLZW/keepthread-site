Frequently Asked Questions

Can I export Claude Extended Thinking without cluttering my Obsidian note?

Yes. KeepThread isolates Claude Extended Thinking blocks into collapsible > [!note]- Thinking Process Obsidian callouts by default, and includes a 1-click Thinking On/Off toggle in the popup when you want to exclude reasoning traces from a specific note.

Does KeepThread preserve Claude Artifact card titles, attachments, and images?

Yes. KeepThread preserves button-wrapped Artifact titles (including multi-span filenames like Dashboard.tsx), records uploaded PDF and file attachment names once without duplicating thumbnails, and archives conversation images locally into assets/keepthread/.

Does KeepThread send my Claude conversations to any external server?

Never. KeepThread parses claude.ai locally in Chrome or Edge, writes directly to your local Obsidian Vault via the File System Access API, and requests zero <all_urls> permissions.
