Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need Google Docs or an API key to export Gemini to Obsidian?

No. KeepThread reads the active Gemini tab locally in Chrome or Edge and writes clean Markdown straight to your Obsidian Vault folder—no Google Docs detour, no API keys, and zero cloud servers.

Will re-syncing a long Gemini chat erase notes I added in Obsidian?

Never. KeepThread merges new turns in-place while protecting your handwritten notes block, custom YAML properties, extra tags, and [[backlinks]].
